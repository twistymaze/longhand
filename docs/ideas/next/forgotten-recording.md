# Stop a forgotten recording

## Idea
A silence check and a time limit show "Still recording?" with a countdown. When nobody answers, Longhand stops the note. At every Stop, Longhand removes long stretches with no speech and a low sound level before it prepares the audio.

## Why
A note recorded for 43.5 hours over a weekend after the user forgot to stop it. The microphone recorded an empty office, Preparing the audio took 13 minutes, and the full audio synced to the user's other computers.
